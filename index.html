ABOUT HAWKINS ARCHIVE

Welcome to Hawkins Archive, a fan-made website dedicated to the mysterious world of Stranger Things.

From the disappearance of Will Byers to the terrifying secrets of the Upside Down, Stranger Things takes us on an unforgettable journey filled with mystery, friendship, and supernatural adventures.

This website brings together information about the characters and seasons, celebrating the story and the unforgettable moments that made the series special.

Whether you're a longtime fan or just discovering Hawkins, there's always something mysterious waiting to be explored.

Hawkins Archive is a fan-made project and is not officially affiliated with Netflix or the creators of Stranger Things.
